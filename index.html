What it is

A personal assistant, built for one person.

JARVIS is a privately developed application that its developer runs on their own Mac for personal use. It is not sold, not offered as a service and not available for anyone else to sign up to. This site exists to describe the project and to publish its privacy policy and terms, as Google requires for applications that connect to Google accounts.

Not a public product. There is no download, no account to create and no public sign-in. If you have reached this page from a Google sign-in screen, it belongs to the developer’s personal installation.

Capabilities

What JARVIS does today, and what is still to come.

Every capability is labelled honestly. Nothing marked “Planned” exists yet.

Live

Natural conversation

Conversations with a refined British personality, powered by OpenAI’s Codex through the developer’s own ChatGPT subscription. Replies stream in as they are written.

Live

Conversation history and memory

Conversations are saved on the Mac. Long-term memories are kept only when the owner asks (“remember that…”) or approves a suggestion.

Live

Voice interaction

Push-to-talk speech recognition on the Mac itself, and spoken replies in a British voice generated locally. The microphone opens only when the button is pressed; recordings are not stored.

Read-only

Google Calendar

Answers questions such as “What’s on tomorrow?” or “Am I free on Friday?” from the owner’s calendars. JARVIS cannot create, change or delete events.

Read-only

Gmail

Finds and summarises the owner’s emails on request (“Find the latest email from BMW”). JARVIS cannot send, delete, label or move email.

Live

Secure local architecture

The application runs on the owner’s Mac and accepts connections from that Mac only. Sign-in tokens are encrypted, with the key held in the macOS Keychain.

Planned

Tasks and reminders. Not built yet; JARVIS does not currently create or track tasks.

Planned

Personal productivity

Daily briefings and routines that combine calendar, email and reminders. Not built yet.

Architecture

Local first, with clear boundaries.

JARVIS runs as two parts on one Mac: an interface in the browser and a small backend that holds the credentials. Nothing is hosted on a JARVIS server, because there is none.

On the Mac

  • Interface and backendThe backend only accepts requests from the same Mac.
  • Conversation history and memoriesA local database, with daily local backups.
  • Speech recognition and voiceProcessed on the Mac; recordings are not kept.
  • Google sign-in tokensEncrypted (AES-256-GCM); key in the macOS Keychain.

Outside the Mac

  • OpenAI (Codex)Receives each message to write the reply, including any calendar or email details JARVIS fetched for that question.
  • GoogleCalendar and Gmail are read through Google’s official APIs, only when the owner asks.
  • Nothing elseNo analytics, advertising or tracking, in the application or on this site.

Privacy

Privacy, in plain English.

  • Read-only Google accessCalendar and Gmail are read, never changed. Email is never sent.
  • Fetched on requestGoogle data is retrieved only when the owner asks a calendar or email question.
  • Not stored as raw dataFetched events and emails are not saved by JARVIS; its written replies are kept in the local history.
  • Shared with the AI to answerThe relevant details go to OpenAI (Codex) so it can write the reply.
  • Email bodies can be switched offOne setting limits sharing to sender, subject and a short preview.
  • Disconnect at any timeAccess is revoked at Google and local tokens are deleted.
